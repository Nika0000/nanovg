---
title: Strokes and fills
description: Style paths with colors, gradients, image paints, line caps, and joins.
---

Fill and stroke styles live in the current NanoVG state. They remain active until changed or restored.

## Solid colors

```c
nvgBeginPath(vg);
nvgRoundedRect(vg, 24.0f, 24.0f, 180.0f, 64.0f, 10.0f);
nvgFillColor(vg, nvgRGBA(30, 64, 175, 255));
nvgFill(vg);

nvgStrokeWidth(vg, 2.0f);
nvgStrokeColor(vg, nvgRGBA(147, 197, 253, 255));
nvgStroke(vg);
```

Use `nvgRGB()` or `nvgRGBA()` for 8-bit channels and their `f` variants for floating-point channels.

## Gradients

Create an `NVGpaint`, then assign it to the fill or stroke:

```c
NVGpaint gradient = nvgLinearGradient(
    vg, 20.0f, 20.0f, 220.0f, 20.0f,
    nvgRGB(14, 165, 233),
    nvgRGB(139, 92, 246));

nvgBeginPath(vg);
nvgRoundedRect(vg, 20.0f, 20.0f, 200.0f, 72.0f, 12.0f);
nvgFillPaint(vg, gradient);
nvgFill(vg);
```

NanoVG also provides `nvgRadialGradient()` and `nvgBoxGradient()`. Box gradients are useful for soft shadows and highlights around rounded rectangles.

Paint coordinates are transformed when the paint is assigned with `nvgFillPaint()` or `nvgStrokePaint()`. Create and assign paints under the same local transform used by the shape when they should move together.

## Stroke geometry

```c
nvgStrokeWidth(vg, 6.0f);
nvgLineCap(vg, NVG_ROUND);
nvgLineJoin(vg, NVG_ROUND);
nvgMiterLimit(vg, 4.0f);
```

Caps may be `NVG_BUTT`, `NVG_ROUND`, or `NVG_SQUARE`. Joins may be `NVG_MITER`, `NVG_ROUND`, or `NVG_BEVEL`. The miter limit controls when a sharp miter changes to a bevel.

This fork also provides `nvgLineStyle()` with `NVG_LINE_SOLID`, `NVG_LINE_GLOW`, `NVG_LINE_DASHED`, and `NVG_LINE_DOTTED`.

## Opacity

`nvgGlobalAlpha()` multiplies the alpha of subsequent shapes, including colors that are already translucent. Wrap temporary opacity in saved state so it does not affect later components.
